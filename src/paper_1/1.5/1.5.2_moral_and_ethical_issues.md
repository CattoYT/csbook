## Approaches to issues surrounding technology

There are mindsets that you can take to approach a specific issue in technology.

### Moral

The moral standpoint identifies what is right and wrong, from the viewpoint of an individual.

| Type     | Description                                                                               |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Moral    | Focusing on what is right and wrong, from the viewpoint of an individual.                 |
| Social   | Focusing on the behaviour and expectations of society.                                    |
| Ethical  | Discussing what is right and wrongg from an external entity, such as laws or contracts    |
| Cultural | Discussing how ethnic groups, religions, and other cultural groups view a specific issue. |

### Automated decision making and artificial intelligence

ADM is the classic form of if else chains to reach a specific case. Essentially, following a flowchart/decision tree.  
The most common are the phone bots, where you press 1, and 2 to get specfic options.

AI is a very wide term. The specification revolves around machine learning (the method in which models are trained).  
The model trains over a large set of data in order to predict/classify data. It learns patterns and characteristics, based off of the dataset.
Since the model is only able to interpret characteristics of the dataset, any potential bias in the dataset will also be present in the final model.
