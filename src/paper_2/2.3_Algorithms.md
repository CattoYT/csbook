# Algorithms
![GitHub last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/cattoyt/csbook?path=src%2Fpaper_2%2F2.3_Algorithms.md&label=Last%20updated)

There are three different types of algorithms you need to know for A Level:
- Searching: linear and binary search
- Sorting: bubble, insertion, merge, and quick sort
- Pathfinding: Dijkstra and A*

>[!NOTE]
You will have come across many of these at GCSE already.
